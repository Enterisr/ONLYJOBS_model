Privacy Policy – LinkedIn Job Filter

Last updated: June 2025

What this extension does

LinkedIn Job Filter reads the text of posts in your LinkedIn feed, classifies each post as a job listing or not, and replaces non-job posts with a random photo of a dog or cat.

Data we collect

None. The extension does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

  • Post text is read locally in your browser and passed to an on-device AI model. It never leaves your device.
  • No analytics, tracking pixels, or telemetry of any kind are included.
  • Nothing is written to any server we control.

Third-party network requests

The extension makes requests to three external services:

  • Hugging Face (huggingface.co) — downloads the AI model the first time you use the extension. The model is then cached in your browser and subsequent uses are fully offline. No identifying information is sent.
  • Dog CEO API (dog.ceo) — fetches random dog photos to replace non-job posts. No personal data is sent; only a plain GET request for a random image URL.
  • The Cat API (thecatapi.com) — fetches random cat photos for the same purpose. Same as above — no personal data is sent.

Permissions explained

  • linkedin.com — required to read and modify your feed.
  • offscreen — required to run the AI model in a background browser context.
  • storage — reserved for future settings (e.g. on/off toggle); nothing is currently stored.

Children's privacy

This extension is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from them.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes we will update the "Last updated" date above.
